# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

[Report vulnerabilities privately on GitHub](https://github.com/firezone/sanstun/security/advisories/new) rather than opening a public issue.
Your report is private to you and the maintainers.

## How disclosure is handled

We work with you privately to confirm and fix the vulnerability before disclosing it.
Once a fix is available for the supported version, we publish a security advisory in this repository.

## Out of scope

- Social engineering or phishing attacks
- Physical attacks
- Denial-of-service attacks
- Reports produced solely by automated scanners without a demonstrated, exploitable impact
- Third-party services and dependencies not maintained by Firezone

## Supported versions

We support the latest release of `sanstun` and publish security fixes in new releases.
We do not backport fixes to older versions.
